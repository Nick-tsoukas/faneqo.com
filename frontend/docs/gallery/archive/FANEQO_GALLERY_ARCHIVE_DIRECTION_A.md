## Thesis

**Your Nights, in pictures.**

The page scrolls like a chronological music-memory journal. A Night is a chapter, not a section header. A Gallery is a physical bundle of photographs, not a content row. Grouping comes from photography, typography, negative space, chronology and rhythm — never from a card.

Three rules carry the direction:

1. **A Night's weight comes from how much of it was photographed.** One gallery earns the largest print on the page. Two make a composed pair. Three make a lead beside two supports. Four or more become an intentional shelf. Chapters deliberately do not share a row height.
2. **Desktop puts the Night in the margin.** The dateline, venue, city, truthful context and a quiet *View Night* live in a journal margin column; the rest of the width belongs to photographs. Every chapter's photographic field starts at the same x, which is what lets differently-shaped chapters read as one document.
3. **Every chapter resolves.** A shelf closes its last row rather than exposing the column count it happened to use.
4. **The lead is the largest photograph in its chapter, whatever the artist shot.** Span is not scale: a support cannot become the chapter's biggest print by being handed leftover columns, and a lead cannot be out-towered by a support that happens to be a portrait.

## Gallery Stack presentation

`FaneqoGalleryStack.vue` is a locked signature primitive and was **not** modified. All premium presentation happens in the wrapper:

- **Print separation.** One hairline between the photograph and its annotation, and nothing else. It brightens on hover and focus-within.
- **Cover hierarchy.** Solo prints get `size="archive"` and a larger caption; supports get `size="compact"` and a contact-sheet caption with the facts on a second line.
- **The solo measure.** The solo plate owns the full field width so its annotation line runs the whole measure; only the photograph is capped (34rem, 40rem at 1600+).
- **Lead scale is a height, not a column count.** A shelf lead owns its whole first row and its photograph is sized by a hero height (`--njp-hero`) multiplied by its own ratio, so a landscape and a portrait lead print to the same height and neither can be out-measured by a support. Its annotation keeps the full measure.
- **One caption grammar at every scale.** State above the name, then the name, then the facts. `DRAFT` never joins the run of facts, and it never pushes the Gallery's title below its own metadata — including on lead and pair plates, where title and count share one printed line.
- **No photographs yet.** A Gallery with no media never gets a large empty rectangle. It gets a small waiting slot capped at 21rem, so it can never claim a lead print. With no count to print, its caption keeps the system's second line by naming who it is for.

## Deliberately rejected

| Rejected | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Section cards per Night | The exact CMS read the direction exists to kill |
| Uniform row height for every Night | Makes a one-gallery Night and a six-gallery Night the same event |
| Closing a shelf row by widening one support | Solves the ragged row by handing a support the lead's measure — a rhythm with no downbeat |
| Cropping supports to a shared ratio | Buys aligned baselines by lying about what was shot; the lead's height already carries the hierarchy |
| Horizontal-scroll shelves on mobile | Basic comprehension must not depend on a swipe |
| Exaggerated tilt, glass, floating cards, large shadows | Locked Stack already carries the physicality |
| A metrics strip in the masthead | This is an archive, not a dashboard |
| Renaming General in product truth | "Outside the Night" is lab art direction only |
| Editing `FaneqoGalleryStack.vue` | Locked primitive; wrappers own context |

## Known open items (not defects, deferred by judgment)

- Caption baselines stagger slightly within a row because prints keep their native ratios.
- Count vocabulary shifts between the masthead ledger (`9 photographs`), the chapter margin (`9 photographs`) and the plate caption (`9 photos`).
- The lab prints `1 photo` where the shared `mediaPreviewLabel` still returns `Photo`. Correct for this page; if the direction ships, resolve it at the util so `/galleries` cannot disagree with it.
- A row that closes on two prints prints them larger than the contact-sheet size above them. Even and full, but the shelf is not one single print size.
- `general` alone still shows the "Your Nights, in pictures." headline.
- Activation is inert in the lab: opening a Gallery or a Night prints a status line instead of navigating.
